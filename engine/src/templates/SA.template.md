## 1. Formation
A Syndicate is formed under the New Zealand Thoroughbred Racing Inc. ("NZTR") Bloodstock Syndication Code of Practice ("COP"), in accordance with its formation requirements, by the Promoter as set out in the attached Product Disclosure Statement ("the Syndicate").

---

## 2. Object
The object of the Syndicate is to lease and race {{horse.legal_name}} as a recreational pursuit, with all syndicate members holding fractional leasehold shares for the fixed term described in the Product Disclosure Statement.

---

## 3. Agreement and Parties
This Agreement is binding on the Promoter (Evolution Stables Ltd), the Syndicate Manager (Evolution Stables Ltd), and each Shareholder as defined in the Product Disclosure Statement. By executing the Application Form, each Shareholder agrees to be bound by this Agreement and the Product Disclosure Statement. This Agreement may only be altered by special resolution (75% of shareholding) and must not increase a Shareholder's liability beyond what is disclosed in the Product Disclosure Statement.

---

## 4. Syndicate Shares
The Syndicate is divided into shares of {{dsl.step}}% each, representing the {{dsl.listed_stake}}% leasehold interest in {{horse.legal_name}} for the Lease Term. Shares are issued and recorded by Evolution Stables Ltd. Each Shareholder's rights and obligations are proportional to their shareholding.

---

## 5. Lease Duration
The lease term is fixed at {{dsl.term_months}} months, commencing {{dsl.term_start}} and ending {{dsl.end_date}}. All lease terms are counted in full calendar months. The lease may be renewed or extended at the Manager's and Lessor's discretion, with terms disclosed to Shareholders prior to renewal.

---

## 6. Equine Welfare Supremacy
The licensed Trainer and Racing Manager hold sole, absolute, and unchallengeable discretion regarding all training regimes, race nominations, trackwork, spelling, and veterinary care. Neither Evolution Stables Ltd nor any syndicate member may override veterinary or welfare decisions. The welfare of the thoroughbred is paramount at all times.

---

## 7. Manager's Powers and Duties
The Manager (Evolution Stables Ltd) is responsible for overall lease administration and NZTR compliance, including communication with shareholders and coordination with licensed professionals.

The Manager may:
- 7.1 Make all day-to-day decisions relating to racing, training, spelling, and horse welfare.
- 7.2 Appoint or change trainers in consultation with the horse's owner or Racing Manager.
- 7.3 Deduct and retain management and platform fees as disclosed in the Product Disclosure Statement.
- 7.4 Delegate operational duties to licensed parties as required.
- 7.5 Provide regular updates when the horse is in training or racing.

---

## 8. Default, Prize Freeze & Forfeiture
An investor enters default if a scheduled payment is missed. While in default, prize money distributions are paused. If the outstanding balance is not cleared prior to the next billing cycle, the investment and deposit are forfeited and reallocated to the horse owner. Evolution Stables receives no financial benefit from a default.

---

## 9. Fees, Float & Billing
Members pay for the duration of their participation under one of two models specified in Schedule 1:

- **Subscription Float Model:**
  - **Initial payment:** 5 months of this stake's monthly payment, comprising 3 months security deposit reserve and 2 months prepaid (the current calendar month and 1 month in advance).
  - **Monthly payment:** {{pricing.monthly_lease_payment_unit_nzd}} per month per 1% stake, billed on the 1st of each calendar month, maintaining a 5-month float buffer, drawn down in the final months of the term.
  - **First recurring payment:** Billed on the 1st of the calendar month following join, paying for Month 3 in advance.
- **Upfront Model:** A single payment covering 100% of the lease term collected at checkout with zero subsequent monthly charges.

All billing operates in full calendar-month units.

---

## 10. Investor Returns
- **10.1 Entitlement:** Shareholders are entitled to {{dsl.investor_split}}% of gross official NZTR racing revenue generated by {{horse.legal_name}} during their active participation period, distributed proportional to their leased stake.
- **10.2 Calculation:** Distributions are derived exclusively from official NZTR gross stakes published on loveracing.nz.
- **10.3 Revenue Split:** Gross stakes are split {{dsl.investor_split}}% to Shareholders and {{dsl.owner_retention_split}}% retained by the Owner. Shareholders receive a clean distribution with no secondary billing or capital calls.
- **10.4 Schedule & Qualification:** Distributions are processed {{dsl.distribution_schedule}}. Qualification requires a Shareholder to have maintained fully paid-up status for two (2) full calendar months prior to the race date and completed required AML/CFT verification.
- **10.5 Payment Terms & Compliance Hold:** Distributions are remitted via direct bank transfer or verified payment method. Where verification is pending, entitlements are held in trust without forfeiture until verification is supplied.

---

## 11. Syndicate Management Fee
A 5.0% Evolution Stables margin and 3.0% Platform Fee are incorporated into the listed price of each stake. No separate management invoices or secondary accounting fees are charged.

---

## 12. Manager Removal
The removal or replacement of the Syndicate Manager is governed strictly by NZTR Code of Practice Rule 22.1. A manager may only be removed by a 75% majority vote of syndicate shareholding or by intervention of the NZTR Board for cause.

---

## 13. Governing Law & Jurisdiction
This Agreement is governed exclusively by the laws of New Zealand and the NZTR Rules of Racing. All disputes arising under this Agreement are subject to the jurisdiction of New Zealand courts and NZTR dispute resolution procedures.

---

## 14. Insurance
This syndicate is a leasehold interest, not equity ownership. Leaseholders hold no entitlement to insurance proceeds. Any policy maintained by the owner is at the owner's sole discretion and expense, and rights are not passed to syndicate members.

---

## 15. Early Termination & Refunds
If the horse is retired or the lease ends early, the syndicate winds down. Once all trailing expenses and care costs are settled with the owner:
- **Upfront Leases:** You receive a pro-rata refund of the lease fee for any full months remaining.
- **Monthly Leases:** Monthly billing stops, and any unused prepaid payments and deposit reserve are refunded.

All refunds are calculated strictly in full calendar months. No day-count apportionments apply.

---

## 16. Transfer of Shares
Shares may only be transferred with the written consent of the Manager and in accordance with NZTR rules, subject to AML/CFT checks. There is no guarantee of liquidity or a secondary market.

---

## 17. Dispute Resolution
Disputes must first be raised with the Manager in writing. If unresolved, disputes may be escalated to NZTR and, if necessary, to independent mediation under NZTR guidelines.

---

## 18. Notices
Notices may be sent by email to the addresses provided by Shareholders. Notices to the Syndicate are sent to Evolution Stables Ltd.
