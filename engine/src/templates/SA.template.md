## 1. Formation
A Syndicate is formed under the New Zealand Thoroughbred Racing Inc. ("NZTR") Bloodstock Syndication Code of Practice ("COP"), in accordance with its formation requirements, by the Promoter as set out in the attached Product Disclosure Statement ("the Syndicate").

---

## 2. Object
The object of the Syndicate is to lease and race {{horse.legal_name}} as a recreational pursuit, with all syndicate members holding fractional leasehold shares for the fixed term described in the Product Disclosure Statement.

---

## 3. Agreement and Parties
This Agreement is binding on the Promoter (Evolution Stables Ltd), the Syndicate Manager (Evolution Stables Ltd), and each Investor as defined in the Product Disclosure Statement. By executing the Application Form, each Investor agrees to be bound by this Agreement and the Product Disclosure Statement. This Agreement may only be altered by special resolution (75% of shareholding) and must not increase an Investor's liability beyond what is disclosed in the Product Disclosure Statement.

---

## 4. Syndicate Shares
The Syndicate is divided into shares of {{dsl.step}}% each, representing the {{dsl.listed_stake}}% leasehold interest in {{horse.legal_name}} for the Lease Term. Shares are issued and recorded by Evolution Stables Ltd. Each Investor's rights and obligations are proportional to their shareholding.

---

## 5. Lease Duration
The lease term is fixed at {{dsl.term_months}} months, commencing {{dsl.term_start}} and ending {{dsl.end_date}}. All lease terms are counted in full calendar months. A new lease is governed by Clause 18.

---

## 6. Equine Welfare Supremacy
The licensed Trainer and Racing Manager hold sole, absolute, and unchallengeable discretion regarding all training regimes, race nominations, trackwork, spelling, and veterinary care. Neither Evolution Stables Ltd nor any syndicate member may override veterinary or welfare decisions. The welfare of the thoroughbred is paramount at all times.

---

## 7. Manager's Powers and Duties
The Manager (Evolution Stables Ltd) is responsible for overall lease administration and NZTR compliance, including communication with Investors and coordination with licensed professionals.

The Manager may:

**7.1** Make all day-to-day decisions relating to racing, training, spelling, and horse welfare.

**7.2** Appoint or change trainers in consultation with the horse's owner or Racing Manager.

**7.3** Deduct and retain management and platform fees as disclosed in the Product Disclosure Statement.

**7.4** Delegate operational duties to licensed parties as required.

**7.5** Provide regular updates when the horse is in training or racing.

---

## 8. Individual Exit, Default & Forfeiture
**8.1 Voluntary Exit (Subscription Float only):** Voluntary Exit is available only to Subscription Float Investors with no arrears and at least five full calendar months remaining after the notice month. The Investor may give Evolution Stables written notice to exit. The next scheduled payment remains due. Billing then stops, and the one-month advance plus three-month reserve fund the following four months. Distribution rights continue while the account is funded. The stake ends at zero after the fifth month.

**8.2 Default & Forfeiture:** If a scheduled payment due on the 1st is missed, Evolution Stables Ltd will send written notice to the Investor during that month. The Investor may cure the arrears by the end of that month. If the account is not restored, default takes effect on the 1st of the next month. At default, already earned but unpaid winnings are distributed to the Investor. The three-month deposit reserve and the Investor's stake are forfeited to the Owner, and future distribution and ownership rights cease from the default date. Evolution Stables Ltd receives no financial benefit from a default.

---

## 9. Fees, Float & Billing
Members pay for the duration of their participation under one of two models specified in Schedule 1:

- **Subscription Float Model:**
  - **Initial payment:** 5 months of this stake's monthly payment, comprising 3 months security deposit reserve and 2 months prepaid (the current calendar month and 1 month in advance).
  - **Monthly payment:** {{pricing.monthly_lease_payment_unit_nzd}} per month per 1% stake, billed on the 1st of each calendar month, maintaining a 5-month float buffer, drawn down in the final months of the term.
  - **First recurring payment:** Billed on the 1st of the calendar month following join, paying for Month 3 in advance.
- **Upfront Model:** A single payment covering 100% of the lease term collected at checkout with zero subsequent monthly charges.

Billing and refunds use whole calendar months only; part months are not apportioned.

All amounts are stated inclusive of GST at 15%. No additional GST is charged and no GST item is added to a payment.

---

## 10. Investor Returns
**10.1 Entitlement:** Investors are entitled to {{dsl.investor_split}}% of gross official NZTR racing revenue generated by {{horse.legal_name}} during their active participation period, distributed proportional to their leased stake.

**10.2 Calculation:** Distributions are derived exclusively from official NZTR gross stakes published on loveracing.nz.

**10.3 Revenue Split:** Gross stakes are split {{dsl.investor_split}}% to Investors and {{dsl.owner_retention_split}}% retained by the Owner. Investors receive a clean distribution with no secondary billing or capital calls.

**10.4 Schedule & Qualification:** Distributions follow this schedule: {{dsl.distribution_schedule}} Qualification requires an Investor to have maintained fully paid-up status for two (2) full calendar months prior to the race date and completed required AML/CFT verification.

**10.5 Payment Terms & Compliance Hold:** Distributions are remitted via direct bank transfer or verified payment method. Where verification is pending, entitlements are held in trust without forfeiture until verification is supplied.

---

## 11. Syndicate Management Fee
A 5.0% Evolution Stables margin and 3.0% Platform Fee are incorporated into the listed price of each stake. These expense rates are calculated against the wholesale lease cost and are additive, not compounded. No separate management invoices or secondary accounting fees are charged.

---

## 12. Manager Removal
The removal or replacement of the Syndicate Manager is governed by NZTR Code of Practice Rule 22.1. A manager may only be removed by a 75% majority vote of syndicate shareholding or by intervention of the NZTR Board for cause.

---

## 13. Governing Law & Jurisdiction
This Agreement is governed exclusively by the laws of New Zealand and the NZTR Rules of Racing. All disputes arising under this Agreement are subject to the jurisdiction of New Zealand courts and NZTR dispute resolution procedures.

---

## 14. Insurance
This syndicate is a leasehold interest, not equity ownership. Leaseholders hold no entitlement to insurance proceeds. Any policy maintained by the owner is at the owner's sole discretion and expense, and rights are not passed to syndicate members.

---

## 15. Syndicate Early Termination & Refunds

**15.2 Horse Retired from Racing:** If the horse is retired from racing, the Syndicate winds up.

**15.3 Settlement and Refunds:** Racing may end before the horse's care and rehoming are complete. Refunds are paid as soon as practicable after the Owner's final care and rehoming accounts are settled.
- **Upfront Leases:** Receive a pro-rata refund of the lease fee for unused months remaining.
- **Monthly Leases:** Billing stops, and unused prepaid payments and the deposit reserve are refunded.

---

## 16. Transfer of Shares
Shares may only be transferred with the written consent of the Manager and in accordance with NZTR rules, subject to AML/CFT checks. There is no guarantee of liquidity or a secondary market.

---

## 17. Dispute Resolution
Disputes must first be raised with the Manager in writing. If unresolved, disputes may be escalated to NZTR and, if necessary, to independent mediation under NZTR guidelines.

---

## 18. Winding Up and Post-Lease Arrangements
At fixed expiry, the Syndicate winds up automatically and no further monthly obligations arise. If Evolution Stables offers a new lease, existing Investors have a right of first refusal. Evolution Stables will email the key terms and a reply date before offering the lease elsewhere. Participation requires a new agreement and Investor opt-in. Post-racing care and rehoming follow NZTR welfare requirements.

---

## 19. Notices
Notices may be sent by email to the addresses provided by Investors. Notices to the Syndicate are sent to Evolution Stables Ltd.
