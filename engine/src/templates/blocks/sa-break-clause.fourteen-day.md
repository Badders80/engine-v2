**Break clause — 14-day notice.** The owner may end the lease by giving 14 calendar days' written notice when the underlying head lease concludes or the horse is retired. There is no buyout. On the settlement date the account winds down:
- **Upfront Leases:** You receive a pro-rata refund of the unused lease fee for any full months remaining.
- **Monthly Leases:** Monthly billing stops, and any unused prepaid payments and deposit reserve are refunded.

All refunds are calculated strictly in full calendar months. No day-count apportionments apply.
